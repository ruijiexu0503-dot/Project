## What failed

- Six article-first membership conditions failed source/title coverage or coupling between frozen content roles and uncertain article ownership. Two CLAD membership conditions passed, then failed reading validation.
- All eight reading-first conditions failed at reading validation.
- Ten reading checkpoints exist. Revalidating their final raw responses with only an audited normalization of already-excluded labels recovers **nine** structurally, without changing a reading flow. The remaining response genuinely mixes body/caption/visual flow roles and remains invalid. These are structural recoveries, not newly verified reading-order accuracy.
- The V1 interface presented all raw N labels, including known metadata, but rejected metadata listed as unsequenced. The prompt/validator boundary was too easy to misunderstand.
- It also asked membership to regenerate a frozen role map and required unknown article ownership to force a source-role change. Those are different concepts and created avoidable contradictions. Missing/duplicate titles and substantive-node omissions are real errors that must still fail validation.

## V2 bounded repair

New opt-in driver: `EvidenceNet/scripts/compare_article_reading_order_v2.py`. V1 code and generated records remain unchanged.

- Membership/reading requests receive assignable content labels; known structural/page-context/unresolved source content remains in explicit context records, without assignable N labels. All original raw sources remain stored and accounted for.
- Content roles are copied from the existing common review and never regenerated by membership. A BODY block may have unresolved article ownership without being retyped. Final eligibility derived from page-context/unknown ownership is exported separately from the original role.
- A source VISUAL recognized as publication chrome by article membership can be excluded from evidence without pretending its original modality was not visual.
- Metadata repeated in unsequenced is moved to an explicit `excluded_unsequenced` provenance field. Unknown labels, metadata inside body flows, duplicate/missing substantive coverage and wrong body/caption roles remain invalid.
- Title validation now supplies exact missing/duplicate title IDs on retry.
- V1 shared role reviews and embeddings are reused byte-for-byte and source-hashed, so no new role judgments are introduced between arms. Old article/reading proposals are not reused as new model inputs.
